# SimSight Companion

Play **The Sims 4 with a screen reader**, on the Mac or Windows, and from your
phone.

SimSight speaks menus, dialogs, tooltips, notifications and what your Sim is
doing: with the macOS voices on the Mac, through NVDA or JAWS on Windows. A
phone app (made for VoiceOver on the iPhone) shows the household, the needs,
what happens, and lets you play: pause, actions, the house, the Sims, the
game's questions.

Made for playing together, a sighted player on the mouse and a blind player
hearing what happens, and for playing alone with the keyboard and the phone.

!!! warning "Alpha"
    SimSight is early. The Mac version is played regularly; the newest
    parts of the phone app are still being tried. **Windows is new and not
    yet tried inside the game**: [Windows testers are wanted](testers.md).

## Start here

1. [Install](install.md): one command on the Mac or Windows, the game closed.
2. Start the game and load a household. The accessibility menu opens with
   **Option+Command+A** on the Mac, **Control+Alt+H** on Windows.
3. Connect your phone: in that menu, **phone companion**. Scan the QR code or
   type the code it says in the app. Then read the [phone guide](mac/PHONE-GUIDE.md).
