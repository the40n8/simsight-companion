# Windows

!!! warning "Alpha: not yet tried inside the game"
    The installer, the phone companion and the speech through NVDA are
    tested on Windows 11. The mod itself has not been played on Windows with
    this release yet. Its Windows parts (speech, keyboard, scanner) come
    unchanged from TS4 Access. [Testers are wanted](../testers.md).

## Getting started

1. [Install](../install.md#windows-alpha) with one PowerShell line, the game
   closed.
2. Start your screen reader (NVDA or JAWS; Narrator is not supported), then
   The Sims 4 through the EA app or Steam, and load a household.
3. Press **Control+Alt+H** for the accessibility menu. In **settings**, turn
   **Scanner mode** on (or press Control+Alt+Space).
4. Wait for "Scanner", then browse with the arrows: Up and Down move in the
   list, Right enters a category, Left or Backspace goes back, Enter opens
   the interactions, Space repeats.

Your screen reader speaks for the mod and keeps its own voice and speed.
Without one, a Windows voice is used.

## The phone app

In the accessibility menu, choose **phone companion**: a page with a QR code
opens in the browser, and your screen reader says a pairing code. Scan the
QR code with the phone, or type the code in the app. The pairing page also
has **Copy link** and **Share** to send the link to your phone.

Then read the [phone guide](../mac/PHONE-GUIDE.md): it is the same app on Mac
and Windows. Settings for the phone link (a fixed address, meeting points,
disconnecting every phone) and **updates** are in the accessibility menu
under **companion setup**; with the game closed, open **SimSight Setup.cmd**
in `Mods\SimSight`.
